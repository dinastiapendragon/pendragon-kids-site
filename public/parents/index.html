For parents and educators

What Pendragon Kids is, and the rules we keep about children.

Who this is for

Pendragon Kids is made for grown-ups to share with children aged about four to eight — parents, carers, teachers, librarians and homeschooling families. Everything on this site is meant to be printed and coloured together.

What we never do

We do not speak to children directly, and we never ask a child for a name, an age, a photograph, a video, a school or any other personal detail. We run no contests or giveaways that collect information about a child.

Our mailing list is for adults. If you sign up, you are telling us you are a grown-up who would like colouring pages and activities by email. You can leave with one click, from any email we send.

Using the pages

The free pages are yours to print at home, in a classroom or in a library, as many times as you like. Please don't sell them or pass them off as your own.

Who makes it

Pendragon Kids is written and drawn by Pete Michael. The nine heroes — Arthur, Kay, Aine, Percival, Lancelot, Bors, Gawain, Bedivere and Merlin — are our own take on the old stories of Camelot, made small and friendly.

Questions? Write to us.

Nothing here is a game or an app. There is no login for children, no chat, no account and no tracking of children. Just pages to print.
